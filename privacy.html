Better 88 Days

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

In short: Better 88 Days collects no data. There is no account and no server, and the app never connects to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your visa details, jobs, logged days, and the payslips, contracts and certificates you add are stored only on your device, inside the app. We cannot see them, and nobody else can either unless they have your unlocked phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code. It does not use your location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to photograph or attach a payslip, contract or certificate. The image is saved inside the app on your phone.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification passes through a server.

Backups and exports

When you create a backup, the app makes one file encrypted with a password you choose, and you decide where to save it (for example iCloud Drive or Files). When you export a documents pack, the app builds a zip on your phone and you decide who to share it with. We never receive these files. If you forget the backup password, we cannot recover it.

Feedback emails

"Report a calculation problem" and "Wrong?" open your own mail app with a draft that contains only the rule number and the rule pack version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete them when the conversation is finished.

Purchases

The one-time Full version purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, Apple ID or name.

Children

The app is meant for adults on an Australian Working Holiday visa and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes everything the app stores. Deleting the app does the same. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Changes

If a future version ever collects any data, we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

一句话：Better 88 Days 不收集任何数据。没有账号，没有服务器，app 从不联网。你填的所有内容都只留在你的 iPhone 上。

app 存了什么

签证信息、工作、记录的每一天，以及你添加的工资单、合同和证明，都只保存在你手机上的 app 里。我们看不到，别人也看不到，除非拿到你已解锁的手机。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。app 里没有任何统计、崩溃上报、广告或跟踪代码，也不读取你的位置、通讯录或广告标识符。

权限

  • 相机和照片：只在你主动拍摄或添加工资单、合同、证明时使用，图片保存在手机上的 app 里。
  • 通知：提醒在手机本地安排，不经过任何服务器。

备份和导出

备份时，app 会生成一个用你设的密码加密的文件，存到哪里由你决定（比如 iCloud 云盘或“文件”）。导出材料包时，app 在手机上生成 zip，发给谁由你决定。这些文件我们都收不到。备份密码忘了，我们也无法找回。

反馈邮件

“反馈计算问题”和“这条不对？”会打开你自己的邮件 app，草稿里只有规则编号和规则包版本。你不点发送就不会发出。如果你给我们发邮件，我们只用你的邮件内容和地址来回复，沟通结束后删除。

购买

一次性购买的完整版由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们看不到你的支付信息、Apple 账户或姓名。

儿童

本 app 面向持澳洲打工度假签证的成年人，不面向儿童。

删除数据

“设置 > 删除所有数据”会清除 app 存的全部内容，删除 app 也一样。你存到别处的备份文件由你自己删除。

政策变更

如果以后的版本要收集任何数据，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页和 App Store 的隐私标签。

購買

一次性購買的完整版由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。我們看不到你的付款資料、Apple 帳號或姓名。

兒童

本 app 適用於持澳洲打工度假簽證的成年人，並非以兒童為對象。

刪除資料

政策變更

如果日後的版本要蒐集任何資料，我們會在該版本發布前更新本頁和 App Store 的隱私權標籤。
