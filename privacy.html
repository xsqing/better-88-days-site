Better 88 Days

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Better 88 Days lives on your iPhone. The jobs you log, the hours you work and the payslips you photograph all stay on your phone, with you.

Where your records live

Your visa details, jobs and daily records are kept inside the app, together with your payslips, contracts and medical certificates. They sit on this iPhone and nowhere else, so only you can see them.

How the app works

Everything happens on your phone. The rules and the postcode list come with the app and are updated when you update the app, so checking a postcode or counting your days works anywhere, even out on a farm with no signal. We run no server, so there is nowhere for your information to go.

Camera and photos

When you photograph a payslip or pick a contract from your photo library, the app asks to use the camera or your photos. The picture goes straight into that job's documents.

Reminders

Reminders to log your hours or upload a payslip are scheduled by your phone and show up right on time.

Backups and document packs

A backup is a single file locked with a password you choose. You can keep it in iCloud Drive, in Files, or send it to yourself, and use it to bring everything back on a new phone. Only you know the password, so keep it somewhere safe. A document pack works the same way: you decide where it is saved and who receives it.

Writing to us

When you tap "Report a calculation problem" or "Wrong?", your mail app opens a draft with the rule number and rule pack version already filled in. Add a few words and send it whenever you like. We reply from our own inbox and delete the conversation once it is resolved.

Purchases

The Full version is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment.

Starting over

Settings > Delete all data clears everything the app holds, and so does deleting the app. Any backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Who the app is for

Better 88 Days is made for adults on an Australian Working Holiday visa.

Changes

If the way the app handles your information ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.
