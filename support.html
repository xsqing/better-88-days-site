How are days counted?

Full-time work: every calendar day in the job counts, including weekends. Days lost to weather or unpaid leave are deducted one each. Part-time and casual work: the calendar days are multiplied by your hours divided by full-time hours (five standard days a week). For example, 20 hours a week against a 38-hour week counts as about 0.53 of each day. Tap "Why" next to a figure to see the rule it uses.

Can the app tell me whether I will get the visa?

No. The Department of Home Affairs decides visa applications. The app counts days under the current rules and lists missing documents. It is not connected to the Australian Government or the visa ballot.

My postcode is not found

The postcode list is taken from the legislative instruments LIN 22/012 and LIN 22/013. Place names come from the Australian Bureau of Statistics 2021 data, so postcodes created after 2021 are missing. Email the postcode and town and it will be added in an update.

When do rule changes reach the app?

Rule changes are released as app updates. The Policy updates page shows the rule pack version and the date it was last checked.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone: Settings > Backup & restore, set a password, then save the backup file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone: choose "Restore from a backup" on the first screen. Restoring does not require a purchase. A purchased full version is restored with "Restore purchase". The backup cannot be opened without its password.

Restoring a purchase

Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then tap "Restore purchase" in Settings or on the "Unlock full version" screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data stored?

In the app on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

天数怎么算？

全职：工作期间的每个日历日都算，包括周末；天气停工和无薪假每天扣 1 天。兼职和临时工：日历日乘以你的工时占全职工时（每周 5 个标准工作日）的比例。例如每周 20 小时、全职每周 38 小时，每天约算 0.53 天。点数字旁边的“为什么”可以看到用的是哪条规则。

app 能告诉我签证能不能批吗？

不能。签证由澳洲内政部审批。app 按现行规则计算天数，并列出缺少的材料。它和澳洲政府、签证抽签都没有关联。

查不到我的邮编

邮编表取自法规 LIN 22/012 和 LIN 22/013，地名来自澳洲统计局 2021 年的数据，所以 2021 年以后新设的邮编还没有。把邮编和地名发邮件告诉我们，会在更新里加上。

规则变了，app 什么时候更新？

天數怎麼算？

全職：工作期間的每個日曆日都算，包括週末；天候停工和無薪假每天扣 1 天。兼職和臨時工：日曆日乘以你的工時佔全職工時（每週 5 個標準工作日）的比例。例如每週 20 小時、全職每週 38 小時，每天約算 0.53 天。點數字旁邊的「為什麼」可以看到用的是哪條規則。

app 能告訴我簽證會不會核准嗎？

不能。簽證由澳洲內政部審核。app 按現行規則計算天數，並列出缺少的文件。它和澳洲政府、簽證抽籤都沒有關聯。

查不到我的郵遞區號

郵遞區號表取自法規 LIN 22/012 和 LIN 22/013，地名來自澳洲統計局 2021 年的資料，所以 2021 年以後新設的郵遞區號還沒有。把郵遞區號和地名寄信告訴我們，會在更新裡加上。
