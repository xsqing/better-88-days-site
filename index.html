Better 88 Days - Visa Tracker

An iPhone app that counts the days of specified work needed for a second (88 days) or third (179 days) Australian Working Holiday visa. It shows which rule applies to each day and keeps payslips and contracts with the job they belong to. It works without an internet connection.

Support · Privacy Policy

Not a government app. Visa decisions are made by the Department of Home Affairs.
