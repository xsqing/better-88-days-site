Better 88 Days

An iPhone app that counts the days of specified work needed for a second (88 days) or third (179 days) Australian Working Holiday visa. It shows the rule behind each line of the count and keeps payslips and contracts with the job they belong to. Counting days and checking postcodes work without an internet connection.

Support · Privacy Policy

Not a government app. Visa decisions are made by the Department of Home Affairs.
